Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Sky Glyde is a mobile game made by 4926 Creative Collect ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. In short: we do not ask for your name, email or location, we do not sell data, and the game does not use third-party advertising or tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress is saved only on your phone. This includes levels finished, stars, coins, power-ups, lives, planes and skins you own, your best Free Play and Daily Run distances, and your settings (sound, music, haptics, instant replay). We do not receive a copy of this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

Instant replay and sharing

Instant replay is off until you turn it on (in Settings, or when the game asks after a new best). When it's on, iOS asks for your permission, and then Sky Glyde uses Apple's ReplayKit to keep the last 15 seconds of the screen while you fly a level, Free Play or the Daily Run. It never records the microphone or the camera, and it never records menus, ads, purchases or Game Center screens. The recording stays on your phone: when a run ends, its last 15 seconds are saved as a clip in the game's temporary storage on your phone, and the clip leaves your phone only if you tap Share and choose where to send it in the iOS share sheet (for example Messages, Photos, TikTok or Instagram). Score cards are pictures drawn on your phone and are shared the same way. We never receive your clips or score cards. Saved clips are replaced by the next run's clip, and deleted after you share them and the next time the game starts. If you choose "Save to Photos" in the share sheet, iOS asks for permission to add the picture or video to your library; the game never reads your photos.

The game also keeps a short Instant replay diagnostics log on your phone (when recording started and stopped, how long a clip took to save, its size). It contains no personal information, it is never sent anywhere automatically, and it leaves your phone only if you open it (Settings, tap the version number five times) and share it yourself.

Apple services

  • Game Center: if you are signed in to Game Center, your Free Play distances (all-time and weekly), your Daily Run distances and your achievements are sent to Apple so they can appear on leaderboards. The game shows your rank among your Game Center friends only if you have already allowed games to see your friends list; that check happens on your phone and we never receive your friends list. Challenges you start with friends are run by Game Center. Apple handles all of this under its own privacy policy. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in your device's Settings.
  • In-app purchases: purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. The game on your phone receives only a confirmation of which item was bought so it can deliver it; we do not receive your purchase history.

Advertising

Sky Glyde shows optional reward ads and a banner on the Home screen. These are house ads for our own brands, such as Expose Em, and are built into the game. They do not collect personal information or track you across other apps or websites. If we add a third-party ad network in the future, we will update this policy and ask for any permission the law or Apple requires before tracking.

Connection checks

The game checks whether your phone is online so it can turn ads, purchases and leaderboards on or off. This check reads your device's network status. It does not send personal information.

Children

Sky Glyde is suitable for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases are handled by Apple, so parents can use Screen Time and Ask to Buy to control them.

Your choices

  • Turn off Game Center in your device's Settings.
  • Turn instant replay on or off in the game's Settings, and screen recording permission in your device's Settings.
  • Turn off in-app purchases with Screen Time.
  • Delete the app to remove all game data stored on your phone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and post the new version in the game.

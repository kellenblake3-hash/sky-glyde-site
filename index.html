Support

Sky Glyde – Arcade Flight is an arcade flying game for iPhone. Tap to climb, hold to glide, and fly through four seasons of 15 levels each: Clear Skies, Stormy Skies, Giant Skies and Frozen Peaks. Collect coins for power-ups and skins, earn up to three stars per level, unlock six planes, and chase your best distance in Free Play on the Game Center leaderboard.

Restore purchases

Planes and skins bought with real money can be restored on a new or reset iPhone: open Sky Glyde, tap Settings, then Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought them with. Coins, lives and power-ups are used up when spent, so they can't be restored.

Progress

Your progress is saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it.
